Privacy Policy

This notice explains how Academix handles information when schools and their communities use the platform.

Last updated: October 30, 2026

01

About this policy

Academix is a school platform for administrators, teachers, students, and parents. This policy applies to information handled through the Academix website and its connected school services.

Schools decide which school records they enter and which staff can access them. The Academix project team operates the platform and supports its services. A school may also provide its own privacy notice with details about its records and practices.

02

Information we handle

The information depends on your role and the features your school uses. It may include:

  • Account and contact details: name, school email, personal email when provided, profile details, and account credentials.
  • School information: school name and contact details, administrator information, logo, and academic term setup submitted during registration.
  • Education records: student and parent links, class and subject details, attendance, assignments and submitted files, grades, journals, and progress reports.
  • Platform activity: sign-in sessions, security and activity logs, notification preferences, and push notification subscription details when enabled.
  • Profile information: details such as a learner reference number, birth date and place, sex, address, contact number, mother tongue, indigenous group, or religion when a school form requests them.

Please provide only information needed for school or account purposes. Do not include sensitive details in free-text fields unless your school asks for them.

03

How and why information is used

Academix uses information to provide school services, including managing accounts and school registration; organizing classes, attendance, assignments, grades, and reports; sharing announcements and notifications; supporting school forms; maintaining security; and troubleshooting the platform.

Schools use education records for their academic and administrative work. Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), the school or Academix determines the lawful basis for each purpose. Depending on the specific use, this may include consent, a legal obligation, or legitimate interests when permitted. The basis can vary by record and purpose. We do not treat reading or using Academix alone as consent for every use of information.

04

AI reports and service providers

Some schools may enable AI-assisted narrative report drafts. When a teacher requests a draft, Academix sends relevant report evidence to OpenAI’s API. This can include attendance, assignment details, scores and grades, teacher notes, and student journal reflections when that feature is applicable. The AI produces a draft for a teacher to review; it does not replace the school’s review.

AI report generation may be unavailable when a school has not enabled or configured it. Teachers should follow their school’s rules before including student information in an AI-generated report.

Academix may also use configured email and browser push notification services to deliver account messages and notifications. Those services receive the information needed to deliver the requested message. Their own privacy terms may also apply.

05

Who can access information

Access is provided to authorized users according to their role, school, and school responsibilities. For example, teachers may access records needed for their classes, and parents may see information for students linked to their accounts.

Academix may share information with service providers that support the platform, including the AI, email, and push notification services described above. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or needed to protect the security and proper operation of the platform. Academix does not publish student records for advertising.

06

How information is protected

Academix uses account authentication, role and school-based access controls, session safeguards, and activity logs to help protect information and detect improper access. Schools are responsible for assigning access carefully, keeping account details accurate, and protecting their users’ sign-in credentials.

No online service can guarantee complete security. If you believe an account or record has been accessed improperly, contact your school administrator and the Academix team promptly.

07

Retention and deletion

Information is kept while it is needed to provide school services, maintain records, meet legal obligations, or resolve disputes. The period can depend on the type of record and the school’s retention rules. When information is no longer needed, it should be securely deleted or de-identified in line with the applicable retention requirements.

For the retention period of a particular student or school record, contact the school that maintains it. Contact the Academix team for questions about platform account or service records.

08

Your privacy rights

Depending on the law and the circumstances, you may have the right to be informed, access or correct your information, object to certain processing, request erasure or blocking, request data portability, and raise a complaint.

To make a request about a student record, contact the school that maintains it. A parent or guardian may contact the school on a child’s behalf. For help with an Academix account or this policy, email the Academix team. We may need to verify your identity and authority before responding.

09

Questions or privacy requests?

Contact the Academix project team for questions about this policy, platform accounts, or privacy requests.

academix.system@gmail.com

This policy describes the Academix platform. It does not replace a school’s own privacy notice or explain every processing activity a school may carry out.
